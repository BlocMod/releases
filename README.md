# BlocMod

A Minecraft block modeller with the feel of Blender, for Windows. Website: [blocmod.app](https://blocmod.app)

## Download

The newest installer is under [Releases](https://github.com/BlocMod/releases/releases). There are two apps, and they can be installed side by side:

- **BlocMod**: the regular builds.
- **BlocMod Experimental**: the newest work, which may break. It keeps its own settings.

## Before you install

- BlocMod reads Minecraft's textures and models from your own copy of Minecraft: Java Edition. Nothing from Minecraft is included. It is made for Minecraft 26.3.
- From 0.3.0, the installer is signed by **AetherCarlyle Ltd**, the company that makes BlocMod. While BlocMod is new, Windows may still say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, check that the publisher reads AetherCarlyle Ltd, then choose **Run anyway**. The warning fades as more people install it.

## Updates

BlocMod looks for a newer version when it starts, and **Help › Check for Updates** looks straight away. Beta builds stop exporting two weeks after they are released; updating keeps you going. Your projects always open and save.

## Free for now

BlocMod is free for now, for a limited time. The free period will end, possibly while BlocMod is
still in beta, and BlocMod will then be sold as a yearly subscription, with a free demo that
opens and saves projects but does not export. Free builds will stop exporting within two weeks
of that. Your projects stay yours either way.

By installing BlocMod you agree to the [beta terms](BETA-TERMS.md).
