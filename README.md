# BlocMod

A Minecraft block modeller with the feel of Blender, for Windows.

## Download

The newest installer is under [Releases](https://github.com/BlocMod/releases/releases). There are two apps, and they can be installed side by side:

- **BlocMod**: the regular builds.
- **BlocMod Experimental**: the newest work, which may break. It keeps its own settings.

## Before you install

- BlocMod reads Minecraft's textures and models from your own copy of Minecraft: Java Edition. Nothing from Minecraft is included. It is made for Minecraft 26.3.
- The installer is not signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Updates

BlocMod looks for a newer version when it starts, and **Help › Check for Updates** looks straight away. Beta builds stop exporting two weeks after they are released; updating keeps you going. Your projects always open and save.
